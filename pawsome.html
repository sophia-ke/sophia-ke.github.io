Pawsome is an AI-powered app that supports new dog owners with personalized feeding plans and health advice. Created for first-time adopters from the local non-profit animal shelter, the Humane Society of Huron Valley, we aimed to simplify and enhance the pet care experience.

Project Objectives

Interviews & Surveys

We conducted 1:1 interviews with 6 new dog owners to understand their early challenges.

Key questions included:

  • "What are your biggest challenges in caring for your new dog?"

  • "How do you currently plan meals and monitor your dog's health?"

  • "What resources do you use to find safe foods for your dog?"

Understanding New Dog Owners

To design effectively for long-term engagement, it’s crucial to understand the evolving needs of users as their experience with pets grows. We surveyed 59 respondents, including new and experienced dog owners and shelter volunteers.

Key questions for long-term dog owners included:

  • "How do you manage your dog's diet as they age?"

  • "What challenges have you faced in keeping up with your dog’s health over the years?"

  • "How do you stay informed about your dog’s changing dietary needs?"

  • "What tools or apps do you use to track your pet’s health and vet appointments?"

User Research

Key Pain Points
Research Finding #1

Confusion Over Dog Food Choices

53% of pet owners feel overwhelmed and confused when selecting the appropriate dog food. Many struggle to navigate the variety of options available, leading to uncertainty about what constitutes a nutritious and suitable diet for their pets. 

Pain point 1

Conflicting pet care information online leaves new dog owners uncertain what to trust.

Pain point 2

Struggle to choose between different types of food, depending on their dog's needs.

Pain point 3

Expensive vet appointments with minor issues sometimes costing thousands.

Research Finding #2

85% of dog owners prioritize health and medical information due to the high costs of vet insurance and lengthy appointment wait times. This strong focus on health underscores the need for accessible, cost-effective resources to help owners manage their pets' well-being.

Feedback From Users

We decided to boost confidence in AI by using clear data visualization and validating accuracy through human veterinary experts. By offering a virtual vet Q&A option, users can double-check if the situation is severe. This approach enhances transparency and strengthens the reliability of AI-driven insights.

Build Trust in AI with Data Visualization and Expert Validation

To validate our design, we tested with 6 new dog owners and gathered their feedback by observing them as they navigated through our prototype.

Design Validation Through User Testing
