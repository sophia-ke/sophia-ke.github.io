TangNing is a Shanghai-based healthcare company that launched HealthLoop during the COVID-19 lockdown to offer remote healthcare for senior patients with chronic illnesses in local communities. With hospitals overwhelmed and in-person visits restricted, HealthLoop facilitated contactless check-ups, ensuring continuous care throughout the pandemic. As a full-time designer on a six-person team, I contributed to an app that connects users with local health hubs, designed in collaboration with an external industrial agency.

​

Healthloop

Project Objectives

  • Simplify Appointment Booking: enables senior patients to navigate and book virtual appointments with doctors easily.

  • Health Tracking & Monitoring: provides tools for patients to track and monitor their health with personalized updates and reminders.

Impact

We streamlined the user flow for booking virtual appointments with doctors, making it easy for users to connect with local HealthLoop facility hubs equipped with health check devices to monitor their conditions when needed.

85%

Appointment Booking Efficiency

Reduced appointment booking time from 30 minutes to 3 minutes.

96%

Positive Feedback

Users felt confident with the app and found the data visualization helpful for health monitoring.

During the COVID-19 lockdown in Shanghai, residents were confined to their communities and had to stay home for months, with only access to community facilities. Patients with chronic conditions requiring regular health check-ups could not visit hospitals due to the lockdown, resulting in a lack of necessary medical care.

Project Problem

An app that enables seniors to easily book virtual doctor appointments,  find nearby HealthLoop hubs for chronic disease management, and to track health monitoring through a dashboard.

Find Nearby Healthhub for checkup

Users can easily locate nearby health hubs for in-person check-ups through an interactive map or list, with essential details such as services offered, ratings, and directions.

Dashboard to monitor health metrics

The health monitor dashboard provides users with a comprehensive view of critical health metrics, including blood sugar and blood oxygen levels, enabling them to track and manage their cardiovascular health effectively.

What happens when users need health check equipment?

When users need access to health check equipment, they can use the app to find a nearby Healthloop hub in their community and book an appointment. At the Healthloop hub, they can utilize designated health check equipment for chronic diseases and consult a doctor virtually, or they can book a virtual appointment directly through their phone for ongoing health monitoring and guidance.

Uncovering Edge Cases in User Testing

We conducted usability tests on our hi-fi prototype with 6 participants to identify system issues and track task completion times. Notably, a 75-year-old participant frequently relied on his daughter for assistance, highlighting the need for family support in managing medical tasks.

"My daughter often assists me with my medical issues, so I want to keep her updated."

User
Testing

My Takeaways

As a natural optimist, I often focus on creating a "happy path" in my designs. This project taught me to consider edge cases, revealing the importance of understanding not just the direct user needs, but also the support systems they rely on.

 

Incorporating the Family Assistance feature filled a critical gap that greatly improved the user experience. Additionally, aligning stakeholders around data-driven decisions was essential in shaping the final product.
